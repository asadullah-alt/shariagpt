# Mudaraba — Profit-Sharing Partnership

## Definition
Mudaraba is a partnership contract between a capital provider (rab al-mal) and an entrepreneur/
manager (mudarib). The rab al-mal provides 100% of the capital; the mudarib provides expertise,
labor, and management. Profits are shared at a pre-agreed ratio; financial losses are borne solely
by the rab al-mal (assuming no negligence by the mudarib).

## Parties
- **Rab al-mal** (investor/capital provider): Provides funds. Bears financial loss.
- **Mudarib** (manager/entrepreneur): Provides skills and labor. Loses time and effort on loss.

## Profit & Loss Allocation
- **Profit**: Split at an agreed ratio, e.g., 60% to mudarib, 40% to rab al-mal. This ratio is
  fixed at contract signing and cannot be changed unilaterally.
- **Loss**: Entirely borne by rab al-mal (financial loss). The mudarib's loss is their time/effort.
- **Exception**: If loss results from mudarib's negligence or fraud, mudarib is liable.

## Sharia Conditions
1. Capital must be in cash or cash-equivalent (not in-kind assets).
2. Profit ratio must be a percentage of profit — not a lump sum or salary for the mudarib.
3. The mudarib cannot guarantee returns to the rab al-mal (doing so transforms it into a loan).
4. The rab al-mal cannot interfere in day-to-day management (sleeping partner).
5. The business activity must be Sharia-permissible.

## Types of Mudaraba
- **Al-Mudaraba al-Mutlaqah** (Unrestricted): Mudarib has freedom to invest in any permissible venture.
- **Al-Mudaraba al-Muqayyadah** (Restricted): Rab al-mal specifies the type of investment or sector.

## Islamic Banking Applications
- **Mudaraba savings accounts**: Bank acts as mudarib; depositors are rab al-mal.
- **Investment deposit accounts**: Fixed-term deposits structured as Mudaraba.
- **Islamic fund management**: Mutual funds operated as Mudaraba.

## Mal Mudaraba Investment Account Features
- Minimum deposit: AED 5,000
- Profit-sharing ratio: disclosed at account opening (e.g., 50:50 or 60:40 customer:bank)
- Profit payment: monthly or quarterly
- Capital not guaranteed (Sharia-compliant — not a deposit guarantee)
- PIDM protection does not apply (not a conventional deposit)
