# Musharaka — Joint Venture & Diminishing Musharaka

## Definition
Musharaka (Arabic: مشاركة, "sharing") is an equity-based partnership where two or more parties
contribute capital to a joint business venture. Profits are shared according to a pre-agreed ratio;
losses are shared strictly in proportion to each party's capital contribution.

## Difference from Mudaraba
| Feature | Musharaka | Mudaraba |
|---|---|---|
| Capital contribution | All parties | Only rab al-mal |
| Management | All parties may manage | Only mudarib |
| Loss bearing | Proportional to capital | Rab al-mal bears financial loss |
| Control | Shared | Sleeping investor |

## Sharia Conditions
1. All partners must contribute capital (cash or assets valued at market rate).
2. Profit ratio is agreed by contract — it need not match capital ratios.
3. Losses must be shared strictly in proportion to capital contribution (cannot be contractually altered).
4. Partners may limit each other's management scope by agreement.
5. Any partner may exit with proper notice, liquidating their share at fair market value.

## Diminishing Musharaka (Musharaka Mutanaqisah)
The most important application in modern Islamic home finance:

**Structure:**
1. Bank and customer jointly purchase a property.
2. Customer leases the bank's share (Ijara component) — paying rent on the bank's portion.
3. Customer gradually buys the bank's units of ownership using additional payments.
4. As customer's share increases, rent paid to bank decreases proportionally.
5. Eventually customer becomes sole owner — the Musharaka dissolves.

**Why this is Sharia-compliant:**
- The bank earns legitimate rental income (not interest) on its owned share.
- The purchase of units uses real sale contracts, not a loan.
- Risk of ownership (fire, market loss) is shared during the partnership.

## Mal Diminishing Musharaka Home Finance
- Co-ownership ratio: typically 80% bank, 20% customer at inception.
- Tenure: 5 to 25 years
- Rental rate review: annually based on market rates (capped — disclosed in contract)
- Units purchased: monthly alongside rental payment
- Property valuation: conducted by Mal-approved valuers
- Early full purchase: permitted, no penalty
