# Zakat — Calculation Rules & Methodology

## Definition
Zakat (Arabic: زكاة) is the third pillar of Islam — an obligatory annual charitable payment on
wealth that exceeds a minimum threshold (Nisab) held for a complete lunar year. It purifies wealth
and redistributes income to the needy.

## Who Must Pay Zakat
- Adult, sane Muslims who are free (not enslaved).
- Who own wealth above the Nisab threshold.
- For a complete lunar year (hawl).

## Nisab Threshold (as of reference date)
- **Gold Nisab**: 87.48 grams of gold. If gold price is AED 300/gram, Nisab = AED 26,244.
- **Silver Nisab**: 612.36 grams of silver (generally lower; used as minimum for modern scholars).
- Most contemporary scholars use the gold Nisab for savings and investments.

> Note: Nisab value fluctuates with market prices. Always check current gold price to compute Nisab.

## Zakat Rate
The standard rate is **2.5%** of the total Zakatable wealth per lunar year.

## Zakatable Assets
| Asset Type | Zakatable? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cash savings | Yes | If above Nisab and held one lunar year |
| Gold & silver | Yes | At market value |
| Business inventory | Yes | At cost or market value |
| Receivables (debts owed to you) | Yes (if likely to be received) | |
| Shares & unit trusts | Yes | Based on underlying assets |
| Sukuk | Partially | Based on underlying asset or periodic distributions |
| Property (for investment) | Yes | On rental income or capital gains |
| Personal residence | No | Not zakatable |
| Personal use items (car, furniture) | No | Not zakatable |
| Business fixed assets | No | Only on liquid business assets |

## Zakat on Savings (Example Calculation)
- Savings at start of Hawl: AED 50,000
- Savings at end of Hawl: AED 60,000
- Nisab: AED 26,244 (gold-based)
- Since both values exceed Nisab: Zakat applies.
- Some scholars use the lower of start/end value; others use end value.
- Using end value: Zakat = 2.5% × AED 60,000 = **AED 1,500**

## Eight Categories of Zakat Recipients (Quran 9:60)
1. The poor (fuqara)
2. The needy (masakin)
3. Zakat administrators
4. Those whose hearts are to be reconciled (new Muslims)
5. To free slaves
6. Those in debt (gharimin)
7. In the cause of Allah (fi sabil Allah)
8. Stranded travelers (ibn al-sabil)
